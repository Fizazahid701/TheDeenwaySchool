Who we are

Education that nurtures the whole learner.

The Deenway School is a religious school located on Main Susan Road in Madina Town, Faisalabad. Our website welcomes families who are looking for a thoughtful educational environment where learning and personal values matter.

We believe education is about more than lessons and books. It is also about developing responsibility, compassion, curiosity and respect — qualities learners can carry into their homes and communities.
